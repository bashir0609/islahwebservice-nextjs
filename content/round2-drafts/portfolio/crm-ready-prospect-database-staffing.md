## Challenge

A staffing firm was expanding into engineering and technical placement and needed a clean prospect database that its team could load into its CRM and use immediately. The brief combined organization research, hiring-signal criteria, role identification, contact verification, and import-ready formatting.

Unlike an enrichment project that begins with a complete client database, this assignment required research across 1,100 organizations with active engineering hiring. The next step was to identify hiring managers and talent leaders at those organizations. The value of the deliverable depended not only on finding people, but also on preserving the relationship among the organization, its observable hiring activity, and the relevant staffing contact.

The source confirms that the database was loaded into Salesforce within 24 hours of delivery. It does not provide the number of contacts, the date window used to define active hiring, the specific engineering roles being recruited, or the staffing firm’s identity. ## Criteria

The account criterion was active engineering hiring. The 1,100 researched organizations were selected around that observable signal. Active hiring should be understood as a client-defined research criterion, not proof that an organization wanted an external staffing partner or had budget allocated for one.

Contact criteria focused on hiring managers and talent leaders. Those role categories were relevant to the client’s engineering and technical placement expansion, but job titles and organizational structures could vary. A researched contact needed to belong to the correct organization and fit one of the requested functions.

The database also needed verified contact data and a structure suitable for direct CRM import. The source specifically identifies Salesforce as the system into which the client loaded the file. Consistent columns and standardized values were therefore part of the practical acceptance criteria.

No required contact-per-account count, field-completion threshold, verification-rate target, or permitted age for a hiring signal is documented. Those figures should remain `METRIC-NEEDED` unless supported by the original project records.

## Method

Research began with the organization set and the active engineering-hiring criterion. Each organization needed evidence of the requested hiring activity before it belonged in the database. The signal was recorded as an observable characteristic; it was not treated as a guarantee that the organization would use a staffing firm.

Within the selected organizations, role research focused on hiring managers and talent leaders. Contact findings were tied to the correct company and current role information. Business contact details were then verified using the checks available during research.

The dataset was organized for direct CRM import. Fields were standardized so the same type of information appeared consistently across records, and the structure was prepared for Salesforce rather than delivered as an unorganized research export. This formatting step supported the client’s ability to load the data without an extended cleanup project.

The firm imported the database within 24 hours of delivery. The source does not attribute that timing to a guaranteed service level or state the exact import procedure, so the claim should remain limited to this project’s documented sequence. Recruitment conversations and placement activity were conducted by the staffing firm after handoff.
