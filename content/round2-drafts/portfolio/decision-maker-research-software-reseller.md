## Challenge

An ERP software reseller targeting mid-market manufacturers needed to reach people whose roles are involved in system selection. Generic company inboxes did not provide the role-level account coverage the reseller wanted. The project therefore focused on mapping relevant executive, technical, and operational functions at each target account and finding direct contact details for the people in those roles.

ERP selection can involve more than one organizational perspective, so a single contact per company would not necessarily represent the client’s targeting plan. The documented role examples were CFO, COO, and operations manager. Together, these roles reflected the requested executive and operational coverage, while the project results also describe technical role mapping. The research task was to represent these role categories accurately without claiming that every person was a final buyer or had sole authority.

## Method

Research started at the account level. Each target manufacturer was reviewed against the client’s criteria before role mapping began. This prevented the contact search from producing isolated names at companies that did not belong in the requested target set.

Within each accepted account, the organizational structure was examined for executive, technical, and operational coverage. The CFO, COO, and operations manager were typical target roles documented in the source. Where organizations used different title conventions, the research objective was to map the relevant function rather than assume that identical wording existed at every manufacturer.

Direct contact details were then researched and verified individually. This person-by-person approach was necessary because a generic mailbox would not establish which person held the relevant role. Each finding needed to remain connected to the correct account and role category.

The mapped records were assembled into a consistent database and formatted for the reseller’s CRM. The reseller’s sales team took responsibility after delivery, including outreach, conversations, opportunity management, and the wider sales cycle. ## QA

Quality checks focused first on account-to-contact accuracy. A valid person without a reliable company match would not meet the assignment, and a correct company with an irrelevant role would not satisfy the mapping criteria. The QA process therefore needed to preserve the relationship among account, person, title, and role category.

Role review also mattered because executive, technical, and operational are functional categories, not interchangeable labels. The documented CFO, COO, and operations-manager examples needed to be represented without overclaiming decision authority. A title may indicate involvement in the client’s target process, but it does not prove budget ownership, active intent, or final approval authority.
