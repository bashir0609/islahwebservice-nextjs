## Challenge

A healthcare IT consultancy serving private clinics and care groups needed decision-maker research across a specialized UK market. Generic practice inboxes did not meet the brief. The consultancy required identifiable people in relevant IT, technology, compliance, or privacy functions, connected to healthcare organizations matching its account criteria.

The project combined organization profiling with contact-level verification. Healthcare organizations first needed to be assessed by size, geography, and IT-related activity. The research then had to locate the IT director, CTO, and compliance or privacy lead where those roles could be identified.

The niche context increased the importance of careful role matching. A general contact address could not substitute for a named relevant role, while a senior title could not be assumed to represent every technical, compliance, or privacy responsibility. The research also needed to avoid converting IT-related activity into an unsupported claim that an organization intended to purchase consulting services.

## Method

The work began by profiling healthcare organizations against the client’s size, geography, and IT-activity criteria. The purpose of this step was to create a criteria-matched account set before contact research began. It avoided treating every clinic or care organization as interchangeable.

For each matched account, research focused on current technology and governance leadership. The specified functions were IT director, CTO, and compliance or privacy lead. Public company and professional information was used to connect each person to the correct organization and assess whether the role aligned with the brief.

Direct contact details were researched and verified individually as described in the original source. A verification date was retained so the client could see when the contact information had been reviewed. The resulting data was deduplicated and standardized for the consultancy’s CRM. Account criteria, role information, contact details, and dates needed to stay correctly aligned. The consultancy retained responsibility for campaign execution and for any clinical-side selling or consulting conversations.
