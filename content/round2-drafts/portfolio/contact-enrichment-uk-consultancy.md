## Challenge

A UK management consultancy was preparing for an account-based marketing push and already had a named account list. Its need was not net-new market discovery. The consultancy needed the existing accounts enriched with fresh contact and company information so its own marketing and sales teams could plan and execute their work from a cleaner dataset.

The source identifies several gaps to address: current leadership contact details, recent hiring signals, funding activity, and technology stacks. These fields sit at both contact and company level, which meant the project had to preserve the relationship between an account and its researched leadership while also adding contextual company information.

Because the list supported account-based marketing, consistency mattered across the complete named-account set. Inconsistent values, repeated records, or stale contacts could make account review and CRM import harder. The assignment therefore included standardization and deduplication, not just appending fields.

## Method

The project began with the consultancy’s named account file. Working from that existing list kept the enrichment task anchored to accounts the client had already selected. Each record could then be reviewed for its current leadership coverage and the requested company-level context.

Leadership research focused on identifying current contact details for the relevant people at each account. The source does not specify exact role titles, so the draft should not introduce them. Contact findings were associated with the correct company before inclusion.

The account research then added the requested contextual fields: recent hiring signals, funding activity, and technology stacks. These details were documented as observable characteristics. They were not converted into an unsupported score or a claim that an account intended to purchase consulting services.

After enrichment, records were standardized so equivalent values followed consistent conventions. Duplicate records were identified and removed or resolved, and the resulting dataset was prepared for direct CRM import. Campaign planning, channel selection, messaging, execution, and sales follow-up remained with the consultancy’s own marketing and sales teams.
