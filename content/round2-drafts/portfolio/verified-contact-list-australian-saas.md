## Challenge

An Australian SaaS company was expanding into local mid-market accounts across Australia and New Zealand. It did not have a structured prospect database and had been buying unverified lead lists that bounced. The project was designed to replace that weak starting point with researched account and contact data built for the company’s selected markets.

The assignment required both account discovery and contact research. The client needed Australian and New Zealand companies, followed by the product manager, head of operations, and CTO contacts at those accounts. The resulting records also needed enrichment, email verification using the checks available at the time of research, deduplication, and CRM-ready formatting.

## Method

Research began by identifying companies in the two requested markets that matched the client’s mid-market criteria. Account discovery and contact discovery were treated as connected stages: a person could only be useful to the project if the associated company belonged in the target account set.

At each selected account, research focused on the product manager, head of operations, and CTO. The goal was to create role-level coverage relevant to the client’s plan without falling back on generic company addresses. Contact records were enriched with the available business information and kept linked to the correct company.

Emails were checked using the verification options available at the time of research. This timestamped limitation matters because mailbox status can change after delivery. Records were then reviewed for duplicates and standardized into a consistent structure suitable for the client’s CRM.

The work produced the prospect database; it did not include outreach, campaign execution, reply handling, or sales conversations. Those activities remained under the client’s control after delivery.
