# B2B Prospect Research and Database Enrichment Services

Islah Web Service helps B2B teams obtain structured prospect data without outsourcing their outreach. The work begins with a defined research brief and ends with an organized dataset prepared for the client’s workflow. Depending on the starting point, that may mean building a new prospect database or improving records the client already owns.

**We Build the Data. Your Team Controls the Outreach.**

This boundary is important. Islah provides research and data services, not managed cold email, LinkedIn outreach, appointment setting, meeting booking, SDR outsourcing, or sales closing. The deliverable is criteria-matched, verified where possible, CRM-ready prospect data. Your team decides how, when, and whether to contact each record.

## Choose the service based on your starting point

The simplest question is: do you need new companies or people discovered, or do you already have records that need work?

| Situation | B2B Prospect Research | Existing Database Enrichment |
|---|---|---|
| Starting point | A target-market brief or research criteria | A client-supplied spreadsheet, export, or database |
| Main objective | Discover new companies and relevant people | Complete, update, verify, clean, and standardize existing records |
| Typical company work | Account discovery, firmographic research, criteria matching | Fill missing company fields, normalize names, correct outdated details |
| Typical people work | Research roles and identify relevant contacts | Update titles, add missing profiles or contact fields where available |
| Email work | Research and verify business emails where available | Verify supplied emails and research missing business emails where available |
| Data quality work | Structure a new, consistent database | Deduplicate, standardize, and flag incomplete or uncertain records |
| Best fit | A new market, segment, territory, or account list | A CRM cleanup, migration, reactivation review, or incomplete list |
| Not included | Outreach, campaigns, meetings, or guaranteed sales outcomes | Outreach, campaigns, meetings, or guaranteed sales outcomes |

Both services depend on clear criteria. A dataset can match observable requirements such as industry, geography, company size, role, specialization, technology information, property type, or hiring activity when those details are researchable. These characteristics can support targeting, but they do not prove intent, budget, need, decision authority, or readiness to buy.

## B2B Prospect Research

B2B Prospect Research is for teams that need a new set of companies, contacts, or both. Islah translates client-defined criteria into a repeatable research process, reviews available sources, records relevant fields, and formats the result for practical use.

A brief might define a market by company type, location, employee range, business model, or another observable characteristic. It may also specify the roles to research, such as founders, partners, operations leaders, sales leaders, HR leaders, IT leaders, acquisitions professionals, or property managers. Role relevance is determined against the agreed brief; it is not presented as proof that a person is the final buyer.

### Typical prospect-research deliverables

- Criteria-matched company or account records
- Relevant person and role research
- Public professional profile URLs where available
- Business email research and verification where available
- Requested firmographic or industry-specific fields
- Source or research notes when included in scope
- Consistent field names and record formatting
- Duplicate review within the delivered dataset
- CRM-ready CSV or spreadsheet delivery
- Clear flags for unavailable, uncertain, or exception records

The exact fields should be agreed before full production. A small reviewed sample can help confirm that the criteria, field definitions, and formatting match the intended workflow.

## Existing Database Enrichment

Existing Database Enrichment is the separate option for teams that already have records. The client supplies a spreadsheet, CRM export, or another structured file, and Islah researches missing or outdated details, standardizes inconsistent values, checks duplicates, and returns an improved dataset.

This service can be useful when records were assembled from different sources, inherited during a CRM migration, collected over time, or left incomplete. The goal is not to pretend every field can always be found. The goal is to improve the usability of the supplied records while making uncertainty and unavailable information visible.

### Typical enrichment deliverables

- Missing company details researched where available
- Current titles or role changes reviewed where researchable
- Professional profile URLs added where available
- Missing business emails researched and verified where available
- Supplied emails checked using the agreed verification approach
- Company names, locations, industries, and other fields standardized
- Duplicate or likely duplicate records flagged
- Invalid, incomplete, conflicting, or uncertain fields identified
- Original identifiers preserved when required for CRM import
- Cleaned CSV or spreadsheet returned in the agreed column structure

Enrichment does not automatically mean replacing every value. Projects should define whether the original value is preserved, overwritten, placed beside a researched value, or accompanied by a status column.

## Which service fits these scenarios?

### “We are entering a new market and do not have a list.”

Choose **B2B Prospect Research**. Provide the target geography, company characteristics, exclusions, relevant roles, and required output fields. The project begins with discovery rather than a client-owned source list.

### “We have target accounts but need relevant people.”

This is usually **B2B Prospect Research** focused on decision-maker or role research within named accounts. The account list is supplied, but the people are newly discovered. Define acceptable role levels, departments, alternatives, and exclusion rules.

### “Our CRM has missing titles, emails, and LinkedIn profiles.”

Choose **Existing Database Enrichment**. The records already exist, so the work centers on completing and updating them rather than building a new market list.

### “Our spreadsheet contains duplicates and inconsistent company names.”

Choose **Existing Database Enrichment**. Agree on matching rules, retained identifiers, preferred formatting, and how potential duplicates should be flagged for review.

### “We have a list, but many companies no longer fit our criteria.”

This is typically an **Existing Database Enrichment** project with a criteria review. Islah can research observable characteristics and flag whether each record matches the specified rules. A fit label should reflect those transparent rules, not purchase intent.

### “We need both new accounts and cleanup of old records.”

The work can be divided into two clearly labeled scopes: prospect research for newly discovered records and enrichment for client-supplied records. Keeping source and status fields distinct prevents the two workflows from becoming ambiguous.

## How a project can be scoped

A useful brief defines the target, fields, sources or source constraints, output format, exclusions, treatment of uncertain data, and sample-review process. Industry terms should be clarified because the same label can mean different things to different teams. Geography should specify whether it refers to headquarters, operating location, property location, or the contact’s location. Company-size bands should identify the preferred measure where possible.

For contact research, the brief should distinguish required roles from acceptable alternatives. It should also define whether multiple contacts per company are useful. For enrichment, the brief should identify the stable record key and explain which original columns must remain untouched.

Research is scoped per project because availability varies by market, field, company, and person. Business email verification can reduce avoidable uncertainty, but it does not guarantee delivery or a response. Data also changes over time, so delivery notes and verification statuses should be interpreted in the context of when the research was performed.

## Delivery designed for review and import

A useful dataset needs more than populated cells. Columns should have consistent meanings, formatting should be predictable, and exceptions should be visible. Delivery can include a primary dataset plus notes or status fields that explain unavailable values, role substitutions, verification outcomes, or records requiring client review.

Before importing data, the client should review the sample, map columns to the destination CRM, confirm duplicate-handling rules, and retain an appropriate backup. Islah can format the research output for the agreed structure, while the client remains responsible for its CRM configuration, outreach decisions, and lawful use of the data.

Data-protection and direct-marketing requirements vary by jurisdiction, data source, communication method, business type, and intended use. Clients should apply the requirements relevant to their organization and intended activity.

## What this service does not promise

Prospect data supports a team’s research and targeting process; it does not guarantee commercial outcomes. A company matching observable criteria is not automatically interested in a product. A relevant title does not establish final purchasing authority. A researched email does not guarantee inbox placement, replies, meetings, pipeline, or revenue.

That distinction protects the integrity of the deliverable. Islah reports researched facts, client-defined criteria, and clearly labeled statuses rather than converting limited signals into unsupported sales conclusions.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you send cold emails or LinkedIn messages?

No. Islah provides prospect research and database enrichment. Your team controls outreach, messaging, sequencing, and follow-up.

### Do you book appointments or guarantee meetings?

No. Appointment setting, meeting booking, and guaranteed sales outcomes are not part of the service. The deliverable is structured prospect data.

### Can you build a list from scratch?

Yes. That is the purpose of B2B Prospect Research. The project should begin with clear company, geography, role, field, and exclusion criteria.

### Can you improve a list we already have?

Yes. Existing Database Enrichment is designed for client-supplied records with missing, outdated, inconsistent, or duplicate information.

### Are business emails verified?

Business emails can be verified where available and when included in scope. Verification status should be delivered transparently; no verification method guarantees delivery or response.

### Can the output match our CRM fields?

The delivery structure can be mapped to agreed field names and formats. A sample should be reviewed before full production, especially when import rules or custom fields are involved.
