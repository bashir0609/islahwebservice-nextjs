# Request a Prospect Research Sample

A sample helps confirm that your targeting criteria, field definitions, and preferred format have been understood before a wider B2B research or database enrichment project begins. Tell Islah Web Service what you need, and the sample can be structured around a small set of representative records for review.

**We Build the Data. Your Team Controls the Outreach.**

Islah provides research and data deliverables. The sample does not include cold email, LinkedIn messaging, campaign management, appointment setting, meeting booking, or a promise of sales outcomes.

## What to include in your request

Share enough detail to make the sample meaningful:

- Whether you need new prospect discovery or enrichment of records you already own
- Target industry or company type
- Geography and how location should be interpreted
- Company-size range or other observable company criteria
- Relevant departments, roles, or seniority levels
- Required company, person, contact, and source fields
- Exclusions, disqualifying criteria, and known edge cases
- Preferred spreadsheet, CSV, or CRM column structure
- Any supplied account list or records that should be enriched
- How unavailable, uncertain, or conflicting values should be labeled

If a criterion could have several meanings, explain the intended definition. For example, geography might refer to headquarters, an operating office, a property location, or the contact’s location. Clear definitions make the review more useful.

## What the sample can show

Depending on the agreed scope, a sample may demonstrate company research, relevant role research, public professional profile URLs, business email research and verification where available, requested firmographic fields, source notes, status labels, deduplication treatment, and CRM-ready formatting.

The sample is designed to test interpretation and structure. It does not prove purchase intent, budget, need, authority, or readiness to buy. A person’s title can indicate role relevance under the agreed criteria, but it does not establish that the person is the final buyer.

## Trust and process principles

- **Scope before scale:** criteria and fields are clarified before broader production.
- **Visible uncertainty:** unavailable, conflicting, or uncertain values should be flagged rather than guessed.
- **Evidence-aware research:** observable facts and client-defined signals are kept separate from unsupported commercial conclusions.
- **Verification where available:** business email verification can be included, with status shown transparently.
- **Reviewable formatting:** columns and values are organized around the agreed delivery structure.
- **No invented outcomes:** samples do not include fabricated clients, performance statistics, testimonials, meetings, or revenue claims.
- **Clear service boundary:** Islah researches and prepares the data; the client controls outreach and sales activity.
- **Client-owned decisions:** your team decides how records are imported, prioritized, contacted, and used.

## Choose the right sample type

Choose **B2B Prospect Research** if you need new target companies, new people within named accounts, or a new prospect database built from defined criteria.

Choose **Existing Database Enrichment** if you already have a spreadsheet or CRM export and need missing fields researched, titles reviewed, business emails verified where available, values standardized, or duplicates flagged.

If the request includes both, label the supplied records separately from newly discovered records. This keeps source, status, and quality review clear.

## Sample review checklist

When you receive a sample, review whether the companies match the stated criteria, whether the researched roles are acceptable, whether required columns are present, and whether status labels are understandable. Check that source and verification fields mean what your team expects. Confirm how duplicates, subsidiaries, parent companies, multi-location organizations, and unavailable values should be handled.

A sample can reveal where the brief needs refinement. That is useful. Adjusting a definition before broader production is better than applying an unclear rule across a larger dataset.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the sample a finished outreach list?

It is a research sample prepared to demonstrate criteria matching, fields, and formatting. Your team should review it before importing or using it. Islah does not send outreach or manage campaigns.

### Can I provide my own spreadsheet?

Yes. If you already have records, provide an appropriate export and explain which fields need completion, updating, verification, standardization, or duplicate review. Preserve a backup and avoid sending fields that are unnecessary for the agreed work.

### Can you find business emails?

Business emails can be researched and verified where available when included in scope. Availability varies, and verification does not guarantee inbox placement, a reply, or a meeting.

### What if a field cannot be found?

The value should be left blank or assigned an agreed status rather than guessed. The sample can demonstrate how unavailable, conflicting, or uncertain information will be represented.

### Do sample records show buying intent?

No. A record may match transparent, observable criteria or a client-defined research signal, but that does not prove need, budget, intent, or readiness to purchase.

### What happens after I approve the sample?

The criteria, fields, formatting, volume, timing, and delivery process can be scoped for a wider project. Approval of structure does not create a guarantee that every requested field will be available for every record.

## Responsible use

Data-protection and direct-marketing requirements vary by jurisdiction, data source, communication method, business type, and intended use. Your organization is responsible for determining and applying the requirements relevant to its activities. Request only the fields necessary for the defined business purpose and review the resulting data before use.

## Send your brief

Provide your target criteria, required fields, exclusions, preferred output format, and an example of an ideal record if one is available. If you are supplying existing records, identify the stable record key and any columns that must remain unchanged. The clearer the brief, the more useful the sample review can be.
