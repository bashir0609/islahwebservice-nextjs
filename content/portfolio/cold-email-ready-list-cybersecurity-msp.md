A US cybersecurity MSP wanted to launch cold email outreach to mid-market manufacturing and healthcare companies — segments with strict compliance needs.

We built a 1,850-account target list with the IT security leader and compliance officer at each company, validated every email with real-time inbox verification, and removed all role-based and catch-all addresses before delivery.

The list achieved a 98.2% deliverability rate. The client's campaigns produced a 4.1% positive-reply rate and 19 discovery calls booked in the first 30 days.
