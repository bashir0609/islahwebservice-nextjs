A US cybersecurity MSP wanted to launch cold email outreach to mid-market manufacturing and healthcare companies — segments with strict compliance needs.

We built a target list with the IT security leader and compliance officer at each company, validated the emails with real-time inbox verification, and removed role-based and catch-all addresses before delivery.

All delivered records were deduplicated and standardized, with role-based and catch-all addresses removed before the client received the file. Outreach and campaign performance were handled entirely by the client's team.
