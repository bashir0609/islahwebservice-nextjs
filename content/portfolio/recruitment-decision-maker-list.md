A UK recruitment agency expanding into contract IT staffing needed direct access to the talent and hiring roles at enterprise organizations — not generic HR inboxes.

We profiled target accounts by tech hiring volume and contract spend, then researched the talent acquisition lead, IT hiring manager, and procurement contact at each one, capturing direct lines and role-specific emails.

The contact database was deduplicated, standardized, and delivered in a CRM-ready format with role-specific emails and direct lines. Hiring outreach and placement activity were run entirely by the agency's team.
