An ERP reseller targeting mid-market manufacturers wanted to reach the executives whose roles are most involved in system selection — not generic company inboxes.

We researched target accounts to map the executive, technical, and operational roles involved in system selection at each — typically the CFO, COO, and operations manager — and verified their direct contact details individually.

Each account was mapped to its executive, technical, and operational roles, with direct contact details verified individually. The database was delivered in a CRM-ready format; the reseller's sales team then ran their own outreach and sales cycles.
