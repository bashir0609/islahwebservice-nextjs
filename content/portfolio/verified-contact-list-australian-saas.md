An Australian SaaS company expanding into local mid-market accounts had no structured prospect database and was buying unverified lead lists that bounced.

We built an account database of Australian and New Zealand companies with the product manager, head of operations, and CTO contacts, enriching records and verifying emails against the checks available at the time of research.

Records were enriched and verified at the point of research, then deduplicated and formatted for the client's CRM. The database was delivered complete; outreach, campaign execution, and sales conversations were handled by the client's team.
