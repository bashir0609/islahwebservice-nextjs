# Positioning — Locked

> This reference defines Islah Web Service's approved commercial positioning.
> It is **LOCKED**. Do not change it unless the owner explicitly requests a
> strategic change.

> **Contents:** 1. [Approved Positioning](#1-approved-positioning) · 2. [Bashir vs. Islah](#2-bashir-ahmed-vs-islah-web-service) · 3. [No-Reposition Rule](#3-do-not-reposition-approved-pages) · 4. [Stop Rule](#4-know-when-to-stop)

## 1. Approved Positioning

### Market category
**B2B Lead Generation**

### Core specialization
**B2B Prospect Research**

### Core work
- Target company research
- Prospect database building
- Decision-maker research
- Contact enrichment
- Business email verification where available
- Company data enrichment
- Data cleaning
- Deduplication
- Record standardization
- CRM-ready formatting

### Secondary distinct service
**Existing Database Enrichment** — applies when the client **already has
records** and wants missing, outdated, incomplete, or inconsistent fields
researched, completed, verified, standardized, or cleaned.

### Core deliverable
Accurate, criteria-matched, verified where possible, CRM-ready prospect data.

### Core boundary
Use and preserve: **"We Build the Data. Your Team Controls the Outreach."**

Islah does **not** primarily sell:
- Managed cold email
- Managed LinkedIn outreach
- Appointment setting
- Meeting booking
- SDR outsourcing
- Campaign management
- Sales closing
- Guaranteed pipeline
- Guaranteed replies
- Guaranteed meetings
- Guaranteed revenue

## 2. Bashir Ahmed vs. Islah Web Service

Bashir Ahmed's personal professional history is **broader** than Islah Web
Service's commercial positioning. He may have legitimate experience with:

- Clay, Apollo, LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- AI-assisted workflows, n8n, automation
- CRM workflows, Python, APIs
- Lead-generation workflows, sales-data operations
- Data entry, data extraction, research automation

**Do NOT automatically turn those capabilities into Islah website service
lines.**

For Islah:
- AI, Clay, APIs, Python, automation, and research tools are **supporting
  methods**.
- The product is the research/data deliverable.

**Never broaden the Islah website merely because:**
- An old Upwork description was broader
- Historical work was broader
- Legacy site content was broader
- An external directory is broader
- An old search-engine snippet contains broader services

The correct framing when tools are mentioned:
> Human research supported by modern data tools and automation.

The output remains: accurate, verified, CRM-ready prospect data.

## 4. Know When to Stop

Do not continuously optimize copy because alternative wording is possible.
When:
- positioning is accurate
- user intent is clear
- the SEO target is correct
- proof is defensible
- UX works
- the conversion path works

mark the page as **APPROVED — NO STRATEGIC CHANGE REQUIRED** and focus effort
on higher-impact unresolved issues.
