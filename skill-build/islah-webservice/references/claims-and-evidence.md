## 1. Claim Classification System

Before publishing or editing any claim, classify it:

### A. Observable Research Fact
*Example: "The company has 240 employees."*
Acceptable when supported by an available source.

### B. Client-Defined Research Signal
*Example: "The client requested companies currently hiring IT staff."*
Acceptable.

### C. Unsupported Commercial Inference
*Examples: "They need an MSP." / "They have budget." / "They are ready to
switch." / "They are likely to buy." / "They are the final buyer."*
**Do not present these as established facts** unless supported by appropriate
evidence.

Observable signals do **not** automatically prove: need, intent, budget,
authority, or readiness to purchase.

## 2. Case-Study Evidence Policy (Strict)

Never invent or estimate a case-study fact merely because it improves marketing.

**Do not fabricate:**
- company counts, contact counts, record counts
- project dates, delivery dates
- email accuracy rates, conversion rates, response/reply rates
- meetings, revenue, ROI
- direct dial counts
- testimonials, client outcomes
- project duration, deduplication counts

**Exact facts may only come from:**
- owner-provided evidence
- contract
- project brief
- delivery spreadsheet
- client communication
- invoice
- verified Upwork history
- existing validated internal record
- other legitimate evidence

**If exact evidence is unavailable → use qualitative wording.**

| Instead of | Use |
|---|---|
| Delivered 1,700 contacts | Delivered a verified decision-maker database |
| 950 property owners, 12,000+ properties | Criteria-matched portfolio owners; property-count data |

Never replace an invented number with another invented number. Revised
(qualitative) wording is the fallback until real evidence is attached.

## 3. Portfolio Governance

For every portfolio item, maintain (where possible) an internal evidence ledger
containing:

- case title · industry · client type
- project source · client brief · project date
- companies researched · contacts delivered · data fields
- verification method · delivery format
- testimonial source · supporting evidence

The internal ledger lives at `docs/case-study-verification.md`. It is
**internal — do not publish**. Do not expose confidential evidence publicly and
do not publish fake screenshots.

Use **"Research Output"** or **"Project Deliverables"** rather than implying
sales outcomes where only data delivery is documented.

## 4. External Proof Rule

When using Upwork statistics or external proof, match the claim **exactly** to
what the evidence proves.

*Example: if Upwork proves 191 jobs, the website may safely say "190+ Projects
Completed." Do not automatically convert that into "190+ B2B Prospect Research
Projects" unless evidence confirms every project belongs to that category.*

Prefer exact defensible wording over stronger inferred wording.

Current sitewide proof claims (keep in sync with the live Upwork profile):
- "190+ projects delivered" — matches Upwork job count
- "100% Job Success Score" — matches current Upwork JSS
- "Since 2016" — matches earliest contract history
- Testimonials — must trace to client messages/reviews authorizing publication

## 5. Sitewide Claim Hygiene

Sweep rules (applied in every audit):

- No guaranteed outcomes (pipeline, revenue, replies, meetings, qualified
  leads).
- No managed outreach/service claims (cold email, LinkedIn, appointment
  setting).
- No need/intent/budget/readiness inference from company signals.
- "AI qualification," "predictive scoring," and "buying committee" language is
  out of scope for the commercial positioning.
- Keep all wording within the terminology reference
  (`references/terminology.md`).

## 6. Legal / Compliance Language

The developer agent is **not a legal authority**.

- Do not create broad statements such as "This process is GDPR compliant."
- Prefer factual, scoped wording, e.g.: *"Data-protection and direct-marketing
  requirements vary by jurisdiction, data source, communication method,
  business type, and intended use."*
- Legal/compliance pages must describe **actual implemented behavior** rather
  than hypothetical behavior.
- When naming vendors or processors (Google, Vercel, Resend, analytics tools),
  verify they are actually used before publishing them.

## 7. Service Routing (Reminder)

- **New companies/people need discovery** → B2B Prospect Research
  (finding target companies, new decision-makers, new prospect database, account
  research, property/company discovery, role research).
- **Client already has records** → Existing Database Enrichment (missing emails,
  updated titles, missing LinkedIn profiles, company field completion, email
  verification, deduplication, standardization, correcting outdated records).

Do not blur these two services.
