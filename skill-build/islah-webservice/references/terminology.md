# Terminology Reference

Use this reference when writing or editing any Islah Web Service copy, metadata,
schema, blog content, or case studies.

> **Contents:** [Preferred](#preferred) · [Use Carefully](#use-carefully) · [Avoid](#avoid-as-islah-service-claims) · [Replacement Phrasing](#preferred-replacement-phrasing) · [Market Research](#market-research-is-not-the-positioning) · [Sales Intelligence](#sales-intelligence-is-not-the-positioning) · [Fit vs. Intent](#fit-scoring-vs-intent-scoring) · [AI Framing](#ai-and-automation-framing)

## Preferred

- targeting criteria
- client-defined criteria
- criteria matching
- research filtering
- fit assessment against client-defined criteria
- company research
- prospect research
- decision-maker research
- industry-specific company research
- observable characteristics
- research signals
- client-defined indicators
- CRM-ready prospect data
- business email verified where available
- existing database enrichment
- B2B prospect research and data partner

## Use Carefully

These words carry heavy inference. When used, anchor them to client-defined
criteria or documented evidence:

- ICP (client's own targeting criteria; do not claim Islah defines "your perfect ICP")
- qualification
- intent
- buying signals (prefer: research signals / observable company characteristics)
- budget
- lead scoring (prefer: criteria matching / fit scoring)
- sales intelligence (not the primary service category)
- buyer
- decision-maker authority

## Avoid as Islah Service Claims

- appointment setting
- meeting booking
- managed cold email
- managed LinkedIn outreach
- SDR service
- guaranteed qualified leads
- predictable pipeline
- guaranteed revenue
- AI qualification
- guaranteed buyers
- conversion-ready prospects

## Preferred Replacement Phrasing

| Instead of | Use |
|---|---|
| Find ideal customers | Identify companies matching your targeting criteria |
| Improve outbound campaigns | Provide accurate prospect data for clients' own sales and outreach workflows |
| Support cold email and LinkedIn outreach | Deliver prospect data clients can use within their own email, LinkedIn, calling, CRM, and sales workflows |
| Lead qualification | Criteria matching / research filtering / fit assessment against client-defined criteria |
| Buying signals | Research signals / observable company characteristics / client-defined indicators |

## "Market Research" Is Not the Positioning

Do not reposition Islah as a general Market Research agency. "Market research"
can imply surveys, consumer research, focus groups, market sizing, market-entry
consulting, customer research, or strategic consulting. Prefer **company
research**, **prospect research**, or **industry-specific company research**.
Use "market research" only where supported by a real historical project.

## "Sales Intelligence" Is Not the Positioning

Do not make Sales Intelligence the primary service category. It can imply
behavioral intent, first-party intent data, buying committees, revenue
intelligence, pipeline forecasting, intent platforms, or predictive scoring.
Preferred description: **B2B prospect research and data partner**.

## Fit Scoring vs. Intent Scoring

Islah **may** calculate a **CRITERIA/FIT SCORE** when based transparently on
client-defined observable criteria such as:

- geography
- size
- industry
- technology
- hiring
- funding
- property portfolio
- required role

Islah must **NOT** represent that automatically as a **PURCHASE INTENT SCORE**.
Behavioral/intent scoring requires appropriate behavioral, first-party, or
legitimate intent data. Do not infer buying intent from generic public company
signals.

## AI and Automation Framing

Broader company history may reference Clay, Apollo, LinkedIn Sales Navigator,
AI-assisted research, Python, APIs, search engines, Google Maps, company
websites, industry databases, and automation workflows — as **tools supporting
research efficiency** on process/About content. Do not create service pages for
them. Do not change the main H1 to "AI-Powered Lead Generation." Preferred
framing:

> Human research supported by modern data tools and automation.

The output remains: accurate, verified, CRM-ready prospect data.
